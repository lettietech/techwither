Why Knowledge Base Software Is Exploding in 2026

Historically, knowledge bases served a straightforward purpose: housing employee onboarding guides, internal process documentation, product manuals, and customer support FAQs. The core challenge? Most of those articles were never used effectively because information was scattered across Slack conversations, PDFs, shared drives, and email threads.

Today, AI is changing the equation by moving the industry from Knowledge Management (where information is stored) to Knowledge Activation (how information is used).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is knowledge base software?

Knowledge base software is a platform used to store, organize, manage, and retrieve company knowledge, documentation, support information, and internal processes.

Why is knowledge base software important?

It helps businesses reduce support costs, improve employee productivity, centralize information, and power modern AI-driven workflows.

What is knowledge activation?

Knowledge activation refers to the process of turning stored static information into actionable answers, automated insights, and workflows using artificial intelligence.
