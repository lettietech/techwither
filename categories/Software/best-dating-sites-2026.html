The Big 3 By Real 2026 Numbers [Not Marketing]

1. Tinder - Best for Pure Volume and Speed

Stats: 75 million monthly active users and $58.3 million in monthly revenue. Still the biggest dating app in the world by a factor of 1.5x.

What's new in 2026: No more endless stack. Tinder now serves curated sets based on your behavior, location, and time of day. Less swiping, more intentional.

AI Features: SmartPick AI learns who you view, like, and message. Photo verification + ID check.

Cost: Free = ∼25-100 likes/day, ads, see who liked you blurred. Tinder+ from $10.99/mo, Gold from $18.99/mo, Platinum from $27.99/mo.

Best for: Under 35, living in a big city like Johannesburg, Cape Town, London, or NYC, want maximum options fast.

2. Bumble - Best for Safety, Respect, and Quality Filters

Stats: 50 million users. Built its reputation on women-first messaging.

What's new in 2026: Bumble completed a full AI rebuild in June 2026. It now has the strongest safety stack: private detector that blurs nudes automatically, selfie verification, video call before meet, and AI that detects romance scams and abusive language.

AI Features: AI icebreakers, profile prompt writers, and compatibility summaries.

Cost: Free works fine. Bumble Premium $20.99/mo, Premium+ $32.99/mo unlocks travel mode and advanced filters.

Best for: Women tired of spam, professionals 25-45, anyone who wants filters like education, politics, kids, drinking.

3. Hinge - Best for Actual Relationships

Stats: Leads on relationship outcomes: 36% of dating-app marriages started on Hinge according to the 2025 Knot study.

Philosophy: "Designed to be deleted." It intentionally slows you down. You can't just swipe. You have to comment on a specific prompt, photo, or voice note.

AI Features: Prompt suggestions, "Most Compatible" daily pick trained on long-term success data, rose limit to prevent spam.

Cost: Free = 8 likes per day. Hinge+ $33.99/mo, HingeX $59.99/mo for unlimited roses and advanced filters.

Best for: 28+, looking for a relationship in the next year. If you are tired of small talk, this is it.
